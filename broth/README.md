# Broth Station

The broth is the foundation of a ramen bowl. This folder collects soup bases and explains how to prepare them.

## Broth styles

- **Shoyu:** soy-sauce-seasoned broth; often clear and aromatic.
- **Miso:** miso-seasoned broth with a rich, savory character.
- **Tonkotsu:** pork-bone broth traditionally simmered for a long time.
- **Vegetarian:** vegetable, mushroom, kombu, or miso-based broth.

These names describe broad styles, not one fixed recipe. Regional and household versions vary.

## Recipe expectations

Every broth recipe should include:
- Yield and serving size
- Ingredients with metric amounts
- Pot size or useful equipment
- Simmering time and heat guidance
- How to strain, season, and store the broth
- Food-safety guidance for cooling and refrigeration

## A practical note

Broth can be prepared ahead, but cool it promptly in shallow containers and refrigerate it. Reheat thoroughly before serving. Follow local food-safety guidance, especially for large batches.
