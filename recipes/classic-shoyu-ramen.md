# Classic Shoyu Ramen

A simple home-style ramen with a savory soy-seasoned broth, noodles, scallions, and an egg. This approachable version uses store-bought broth to keep the recipe manageable for beginners.

> **Serves:** 2  
> **Prep time:** 10 minutes  
> **Cook time:** 15 minutes  
> **Total time:** About 25 minutes

## Ingredients

### Broth and seasoning
- 600 ml chicken or vegetable stock
- 2 tbsp (30 ml) soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil (optional)
- 1 small garlic clove, finely grated (optional)
- 1 tsp finely grated ginger (optional)

### Noodles and toppings
- 2 portions ramen noodles (about 180–220 g fresh noodles, or use package directions)
- 2 eggs
- 2 spring onions / scallions, thinly sliced
- 100–150 g cooked chashu or other cooked protein (optional)
- Nori sheets, bamboo shoots, or corn, to taste (optional)

## Equipment

A saucepan, a pot for noodles, a ladle, and two serving bowls.

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the eggs.** Lower the eggs into boiling water and cook for about 6–7 minutes for a jammy yolk, adjusting for egg size and preference. Transfer to cold water, cool, and peel carefully.
2. **Season the broth.** Add the stock, soy sauce, and optional garlic, ginger, and sesame oil to a saucepan. Bring to a gentle simmer and taste. Add more stock if it is too salty.
3. **Cook the noodles.** Cook the noodles in a separate pot according to their package directions. Drain well. Cooking them separately helps keep the broth from becoming starchy.
4. **Warm the toppings.** If using cooked meat, heat it thoroughly. Prepare the scallions, nori, and other toppings.
5. **Build the bowls.** Divide the hot broth between two bowls. Add noodles, halve the eggs, and arrange the toppings.
6. **Serve immediately.** Taste the broth before adding extra soy sauce; toppings and noodle water can affect the final balance.

## Substitutions

- Use vegetable stock and omit meat for a meat-free bowl; check that the stock and toppings are vegetarian.
- Use tamari labeled gluten-free if avoiding wheat, and check the noodles and every other ingredient.
- If ramen noodles are unavailable, use another wheat noodle. Texture will differ.

## Allergens

Soy and wheat are likely present; egg, sesame, and other allergens depend on the noodles, stock, and toppings. Check package labels and prevent cross-contact.

## Food safety and storage

Cook eggs and any meat safely. Refrigerate leftovers promptly in separate airtight containers and reheat the broth thoroughly. Noodles are best cooked fresh; follow local food-safety guidance for storage times.
