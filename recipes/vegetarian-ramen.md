# Vegetarian Miso Ramen

A cozy miso ramen built around mushrooms and vegetable stock. Check every ingredient label: some miso soup bases, stocks, and toppings contain fish or other animal-derived ingredients.

> **Serves:** 2  
> **Prep time:** 10 minutes  
> **Cook time:** 20 minutes  
> **Total time:** About 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 600 ml vegetable stock
- 2 tbsp (about 35–40 g) miso paste, to taste
- 150 g mushrooms, sliced
- 1 tsp neutral oil
- 1 tsp grated ginger (optional)
- 2 portions ramen noodles (check that they contain no egg if needed)
- 2 spring onions / scallions, sliced
- 100 g sweetcorn, optional
- Nori, sesame seeds, or tofu, optional

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the mushrooms and cook until softened and lightly browned.
2. Add the vegetable stock and optional ginger. Bring to a gentle simmer for about 8–10 minutes.
3. Put the miso in a small bowl, whisk in a ladleful of hot broth until smooth, then stir it back into the pot. Avoid a hard boil after adding miso to preserve its aroma.
4. Cook the noodles in a separate pot according to the package directions. Drain well.
5. Divide the noodles between two bowls and ladle over the broth and mushrooms.
6. Finish with scallions, corn, nori, sesame seeds, or tofu as desired.

## Substitutions

- Use different mushrooms or add leafy greens.
- Use gluten-free noodles and a suitable gluten-free miso only after checking all labels.
- For a vegan bowl, verify that the noodles, miso, stock, and toppings contain no animal-derived ingredients.

## Allergens

Miso commonly contains soy; noodles may contain wheat and sometimes egg. Sesame and other allergens depend on optional toppings and product labels.

## Food safety and storage

Refrigerate leftovers promptly in sealed containers. Store noodles separately from broth when possible and reheat the broth thoroughly before serving.
