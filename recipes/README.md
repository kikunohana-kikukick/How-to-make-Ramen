## How to read a recipe

- **Prep time** is hands-on preparation.
- **Cook time** is time spent cooking.
- **Total time** includes the main preparation and cooking steps but may not include optional overnight marinades.
- Amounts are estimates; noodle brands and saltiness vary. Taste and adjust carefully.
